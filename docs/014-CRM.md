# 014 — CRM
**KVJ Analytics Platform V3 · Phase 4 · Business Modules**
Turns leads (009) into managed relationships and revenue. **[to add]** — net-new, built additively
over existing `leads`/`inquiries`/`orders`. Consumes forms (009), enrollments (LMS), orgs (013).

---

## 1. Objects (010 tables)
`contacts` (person: name, email, phone, profession, org?, source, UTM, tags, status) ·
`organizations` (company/college — shared with 013) · `opportunities` (deal: contact/org, type
[corporate/individual/course], value, stage, owner, expected close) · `pipelines`/`stages` ·
`activities` (calls/emails/meetings/notes, timestamped) · `tasks` (follow-ups, due date, owner).
Leads (009) normalize into `contacts` + optionally an `opportunity`.

---

## 2. Pipeline
Kanban of stages (New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal → Won/Lost), drag between stages, per-stage
value totals, owner assignment. Distinct pipelines for **Corporate/Institution** (agreement →
vouchers → onboarding, LMS) vs **Individual Training** (enquiry → schedule → payment → assignment)
vs **Course sales** (mostly self-serve via Razorpay; CRM tracks high-value/assisted).

---

## 3. Lead → deal flow
Form submit (009) → `contact` (de-duped by email/phone) → auto-reply + internal notify (019) → lands
in pipeline as New → owner works it via activities/tasks → converts to enrollment/voucher/order.
Everything time-stamped; full contact timeline (submissions, emails, meetings, purchases, course
progress) on one screen.

---

## 4. Views & features
Contact list (filter by source/status/tag/owner, search) · contact detail (timeline + deals + tasks +
org) · pipeline board · task list ("my open tasks", due/overdue) · organization view (members,
vouchers, deals, learners' progress) · reports (§6). Meetings/calendar + email logging via 019.

---

## 5. AI CRM insights (018/008) — human-in-the-loop
Lead scoring (fit/engagement) · next-best-action suggestions · churn/at-risk flags · summarized
contact history · draft follow-up emails (admin approves before send). Advisory only; never
auto-contacts without approval.

---

## 6. Reporting (feeds 016)
Leads by source/campaign, conversion by stage, win rate, cycle time, revenue by type, owner
performance, org rollups. Growth OS (008) uses these for recommendations.

---

## 7. Security & scope (012/013)
Permission-gated (`lead.*`, `crm.manage`); org-scoped so Org/Corporate roles see only their org;
PII minimized + protected (012 §8); all changes audited. Never expose one org's data to another.

---

## 8. Build order
1. `contacts`/`organizations` + normalize 009 leads into them (de-dupe).
2. Pipeline board + opportunities + stages.
3. Activities/tasks + contact timeline.
4. Org view + voucher/enrollment linkage (LMS/013).
5. Reports → 016; AI insights → 018 (approval-gated).

---

## 9. Definition of done
Leads auto-normalize to contacts (de-duped, source/UTM) · pipelines for each business motion ·
full contact timeline · tasks/activities · org-scoped + permission-gated + audited · reports feed
analytics · AI insights advisory + approval-gated.
